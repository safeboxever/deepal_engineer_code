Mazda 6E (EZ-6) & Deepal S07 Engineering Code

Generates the engineering-menu password from the last 4 digits of your VIN and the date in China.

⚠️ Use at your own risk. Too many wrong attempts may block the password prompt. Don't go over the attempt limit: try the main code first, and wait before trying more.

How to use

  1. Enter the last 4 digits of your VIN. The date defaults to today in China time, which is the time the car uses.
  2. Open the engineering-menu password prompt on the head unit. On the Deepal S07, you open it by tapping the clock in the top-right corner 10 times. The Mazda 6E (EZ-6) should work the same way.
  3. Enter the main code. If it fails, try the alternatives, then the codes for the day before and the day after. Try only a few codes at a time, because too many wrong attempts may block the prompt.

Codes are valid for one day in China time. Use at your own risk. Changing engineering settings can affect your vehicle and its warranty.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
